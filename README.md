# STI Awareness & School Sexuality Education Policy

## Problem
Adolescents (15–19 years) account for a significant share of new STI cases globally, yet access to school‑based comprehensive sexuality education (CSE) remains uneven. The link between CSE policies and adolescent STI outcomes is not well documented in cross‑country comparative research.

## Research Question
Do countries with mandatory CSE have lower STI incidence rates among 15–19‑year‑olds compared to countries without CSE or with only partial integration of sexuality topics?

## Data Sources

**STI data**  
Global Burden of Disease Study 2023 (GBD 2023), IHME  
- Indicator: STI incidence
- Measure: Rate per 100,000  
- Age: 15–19 years  
- Sex: Both (male / female)  
- Year: 2023

**CSE policy data**  
UNESCO PEER country profiles (Comprehensive Sexuality Education theme), 2024  
- Extracted manually from country profiles  
- Variables: CSE mandatory (yes/no/partial), start level, STI content in curriculum, teacher training, school‑based contraception access

## Methods
1. Manual extraction of CSE policy indicators from PEER profiles → structured table  
2. Download of STI incidence rates via GBD Results Tool  
3. Merging by country  
4. Comparison of CSE status vs STI rates
